🧠Data Analyst: Deep-Dive Analytical Pillars

Moving beyond basic descriptive metrics, our exploration of Olist's 100k+ marketplace dataset unveils four foundational pillars critical for enterprise profitability:

📊 1. Cohort Retention & Lifetime Value (LTV)

While macro-GMV scaled smoothly across 2017–2018, person-level analysis (`customer_unique_id`) reveals that repeat-purchase frequency is hyper-fragile. A single logistics failure permanently breaks customer trust, terminating repeat conversion and destroying long-term customer equity.

⚖️ 2. Asymmetric Cost-Benefit Tuning

Standard classification models fail on imbalanced supply chain data. By implementing an asymmetric threshold of 0.2867, we prioritize catching False Negatives. The cost of a False Positive (minor buffer allocation) is negligible compared to a False Negative (silent 1-star review and churn).

🗺️ 3. Spatial Density & Micro-Corridors

A blanket R$ 4.5M network-wide CapEx distorts payback periods. However, when surgically deployed as regional micro-fulfillment nodes along high-failure interstate lanes (São Paulo to Northeast), capital efficiency multiplies and transit delays compress sharply.

💬 4. NLP Review Sentiment Extension

Over 58% of customer reviews contain textual feedback (`review_comment_message`). Our architecture is extensible to NLP sentiment parsing, automatically classifying unstructured root causes—separating carrier mishandling from seller packing delays.
